UK Advisory Services

Hermetic Labs Ltd provides specialist advisory services to support NHS and public sector organisations in the safe, effective adoption of artificial intelligence.

🔍

AI Readiness Assessment

Evaluate organisational readiness for AI adoption — infrastructure, data maturity, governance frameworks, and workforce capability.

⚖️

AI Governance Advisory

Design and implement governance structures for responsible AI deployment, including ethics boards, approval workflows, and oversight mechanisms.

🛡️

Model Assurance & Validation

Independent review and validation of AI/ML models for safety, bias, performance, and regulatory compliance in clinical and operational contexts.

📋

DPIA & DTAC Support

Prepare and review Data Protection Impact Assessments and Digital Technology Assessment Criteria submissions for AI-enabled health technologies.

🔌

Integration Architecture

Design interoperable, standards-compliant integration architectures for AI systems within existing NHS IT infrastructure and clinical workflows.

🚀

Implementation Planning

End-to-end implementation planning including service design, change management, training, and post-deployment monitoring strategies.

🏥

NHS Innovation Programme Participant

Actively supported through the Yorkshire & Humber NHS Innovation Programme for AI adoption in healthcare.
