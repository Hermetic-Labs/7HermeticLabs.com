One viewport

Keep the place in view while the story gets clearer.

Plans, questions, evidence, conditions, interviews, and outcomes should not live in disconnected portals. Data Direct is designed to keep them attached to the project and reveal detail progressively.

01

Start with the place

A scale-conscious map and model provide shared context. Precision is shown only when the underlying geometry supports it.

02

Open the evidence

Every meaningful claim can lead back to its source, date, jurisdiction, version, responsible party, and current status.

03

Follow what changes

Questions become cases; commitments gain owners and deadlines; evidence closes—or reopens—the loop.

Capacity scaling

The base holds until scale becomes the larger number.

Standard pricing begins scaling above approximately 1.2 GW. The applicable price is always the greater of the base deployment or the capacity calculation.

Initial deployment Greater of $3.5M or $3,000 × scoped MW
Annual stewardship Greater of $1.5M / year or $1,250 × commissioned MW

Built to travel without losing the truth

Local by default. Extensible by design.

Data Direct is designed around a neutral campus model while keeping each project’s facts, permissions, measurements, and local authority inside its own boundary.

Accuracy over decorationMaps, models, measurements, and maturity states disclose their limits.
Progressive disclosureThe primary view stays calm; detail appears when a person asks for it.
Inspectable derivationA question, finding, or summary can show how it was produced and reviewed.
Modular connectionsCards, APIs, QR links, interviews, and future phone routes attach through scoped interfaces.
Versioned accountabilityProposed, adopted, contracted, implemented, verified, and superseded are never collapsed into one state.
