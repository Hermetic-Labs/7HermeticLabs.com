The Premise

Azure Quantum provides access to quantum simulators that can model up to 50 qubits — enough to explore quantum algorithms, test circuit designs, and experiment with hybrid classical-quantum workflows. Beyond 50 qubits, you need hardware from vendors like Quantinuum, PASQAL, or Rigetti.

HermetiQ lives in that pre-stage — the playing-with-it stage. We're documenting how quantum simulation interacts with our orchestration layer, testing Q# scripts against real simulator backends, and exploring what quantum advantage looks like for local-first systems.
