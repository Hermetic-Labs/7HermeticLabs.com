A Safety-First Operating System

EVE-OS is the core platform developed by Hermetic Labs. It is a local-first, safety-oriented operating system designed for high-stakes and regulated environments where reliability, transparency, and human oversight are essential.

EVE-OS was created to support real-world decision-making — not abstract experimentation. It is built to operate under constraint, maintain accountability, and remain dependable even when infrastructure, connectivity, or conditions are imperfect.

Decision Integrity

  • Sovereign execution: completely local processing, eliminating third-party dependency.
  • Zero telemetry: no telemetry, usage tracking, or leaks of operational data.
  • Hardened runtime: read-only root filesystems and cryptographic identity verification.

Operational Resilience

  • Local-first state: offline-first synchronization with conflict resolution.
  • Lean footprint: optimized container images for slow, high-latency networks.
  • Fail-safe recovery: dual-boot partition rollbacks and automated self-healing.

Verifiable Governance

  • Policy engines: declarative safety and operational boundaries.
  • Structured logs: cryptographic audit trails of all system decisions.
  • Human oversight: interactive confirmation checkpoints for high-risk operations.
