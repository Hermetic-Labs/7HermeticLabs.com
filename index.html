Roadside systems · product and operations

One roadside lab. Two distinct products.

Jumbo Jim brings field experience into both the professional vehicle-entry tool and the dispatch desk.

Physical product · deliberate design

Jumbo Jim

A professional vehicle-lockout tool shaped by nearly fifteen years of roadside-assistance field experience. Controlled cable actuation, indexed articulation, interchangeable working tips, and a protective door interface give operators precise control, while tip-level imaging and illumination provide a live view of the lock area on the integrated display.

jumbo-jim.com →
AI-enabled roadside dispatching

Jumbo Jim Dispatching

An AI-assisted dispatching service built on 14 years of roadside operations experience. Job intake and assignment, driver and customer communication, invoicing, billing, activity logs, and modern dispatch controls come together in one operational workspace.

Core platform

A safety-first operating system for work that cannot drift.

EVE-OS is the local-first foundation behind governed Hermetic Labs systems. It keeps execution close to the work, makes connections explicit, and preserves the evidence needed for human review—even when infrastructure or connectivity is imperfect.

⌂

Local runtime

Keep essential processing and state close to the people and systems that depend on it, with hardened execution and recovery paths.

↔

Controlled connections

Move only approved, minimized information through explicit interfaces, with offline-first synchronization when connections return.

✓

Policy and oversight

Apply declared boundaries, preserve traceable records, and keep accountable people at consequential checkpoints.
